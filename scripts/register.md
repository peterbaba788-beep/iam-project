# IAM Project Risk Register

## Project Information

**Project:** Identity and Access Management (IAM) System Deployment

**Purpose:** To identify, assess and manage risks that could affect project delivery, security, cost, schedule and quality.

## Risk Assessment Scale

- **Likelihood:** Low, Medium or High.
- **Impact:** Low, Medium or High.
- **Status:** Open, In Progress, Mitigated or Closed.

## Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Responsible Role | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R001 | Incorrect access permissions could allow unauthorised access. | Medium | High | Define role-based access permissions and review access assignments before deployment. | Security Lead | Open |
| R002 | Exposure of sensitive user information could affect confidentiality. | Medium | High | Apply appropriate access restrictions, encryption and security testing. | Security Lead | Open |
| R003 | Integration problems could delay deployment. | Medium | Medium | Identify integration dependencies and perform testing before implementation. | Technical Lead | Open |
| R004 | Deployment delays could affect the project schedule. | Medium | Medium | Monitor milestones, identify dependencies and maintain a contingency plan. | Project Manager | Open |
| R005 | Users may have difficulty adapting to the new IAM system. | Medium | Medium | Prepare user guidance, communication and training activities. | Change Manager | Open |

## Review and Monitoring

The project team should review this register regularly, update risk assessments when circumstances change, and record mitigation progress.

The likelihood, impact, assigned roles and status must be reviewed and agreed by the group. The entries above are initial examples, not verified project findings.
